A minimalist flat vector app icon: a stylised crocodile head in profile, facing right,
enclosed by a thick rounded-rectangle outline frame that touches the edges of the canvas.
Single solid colour on a fully transparent background. No gradients, no shading, no second
colour, no outline strokes in another colour, no text, no letters, no background shape fill,
no drop shadow, no 3D.

Even, chunky line weights. Large simple geometric forms: a long blocky snout, one round eye
cut out of the head as negative space, and a row of a few big triangular teeth or back scutes.
Generous internal padding between the crocodile and the frame. Strictly centred, perfectly
symmetrical framing.

The mark must stay legible when scaled down to 16x16 pixels, so use very few shapes and keep
every gap wide. Style reference: modern monoline logo marks with rounded corners, in the
spirit of a single-colour glyph enclosed by a rounded-rectangle border.
